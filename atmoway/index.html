Exact GeoJSON polygon intersection against official MeteoAlarm (Europe), NWS CAP (US), and ECCC CAP (Canada) severe weather alerts for both saved places and active route corridors.

Level 4 Multi-Model NWP Consensus

Forecasts from 90 minutes out to 10 days combining multiple weather models with an explicit agreement percentage score so you know how confident the forecast is.
