English

Tap and clear the board. Bigger ones hop smaller ones. 2000 levels, 100 worlds.

Hop Out is an animal puzzle. Tap one and it runs the way it is facing, and it does not stop until it leaves the board, hops, or crashes. A bigger animal hops a smaller one. The same size, or anything bigger in front, crashes and costs a heart. You have three hearts. The board clears when you tap the animals in the right order.

The exit waits. Order decides.

There are 2000 levels in 100 worlds, twenty in each. Every level is its own board. The last level of each world is a Blitz, against the clock. You keep the stars and the medals.

On the first board, look for the animal with a clear path and tap it. Later, new pieces come in: ice that turns you, mud that cannot be hopped, gates of the same colour, a tornado that sets you down and leaves you standing, sleepers that wake only if another animal hops them, a key for the fence, a decoy, a floor pad, an arrow that lets you through one way, and a patrol that moves after you have already moved. Trees, rocks and houses cannot be hopped. Go around them, or move the animal that opens the gap first.

The first world fits in a short break. World 100 takes much longer. Download Hop Out.
