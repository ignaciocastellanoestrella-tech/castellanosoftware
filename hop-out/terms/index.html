Terms of Service (English)

1. The game

Hop Out is an animal puzzle for Android. There is no Hop Out account and no in-app purchases. The game is provided as it is.

2. One campaign per account

On Android, the cloud copy uses the Google account that signs in, through Google Play Games. Each account has one campaign. The game has no list of profiles. If several accounts sign in on the same phone, each keeps its own campaign, on the phone and in Play Games.

3. Two devices

If you play on two devices with the same account, the campaign that has gone further is kept. If both move forward offline, the one that cleared more levels remains. Stars and medals that exist only on the shorter save are not copied. If you do not sign in, the campaign stays on that device.

4. Ads

Ads are shown by Google. The privacy policy explains what data is processed.

5. The save on the device

Clearing the app’s data deletes the local campaigns of the accounts that played on that phone. Each account’s Play Games copy remains. The developer cannot restore a campaign that was never uploaded.

6. Age

Hop Out is not directed at children under 13.

7. Changes

These terms are updated on this page. The date above is the current version.
